# TikPort

**TikTok on your Roku.**

TikPort is an unofficial TikTok viewer for Roku devices, bringing TikTok-style video browsing and playback to your TV.

<escape>**Version:** v0.1.0  
**Status:** In Development</escape>

---

## Features

- **For You Feed** – Browse TikTok videos on your Roku.
- **Video Playback** – Watch TikTok videos directly on your TV.
- **Creator Information** – View video creator information and descriptions.
- **Search** – Search for TikTok videos.
- **Roku Remote Navigation** – Navigate TikPort using your Roku remote.
- **Related Videos** – Discover additional videos as you browse.

Some features are still being developed and may not work reliably.

---

## Installation

1. Download `TikPort.zip` from the Releases section.
2. Enable Developer Mode on your Roku device.
3. Open the Roku Developer Application Installer in your computer's browser.
4. Upload `TikPort.zip`.
5. Install the application.
6. Return to your Roku home screen and launch TikPort.

That's it! Enjoy TikTok on your TV.

---

## Known Issues

TikPort is currently under active development. The following issues are known:

- **Freezes when Searching** – The application may freeze when attempting to search for videos.
- **Only 2 Videos** – The For You feed currently returns only two videos, even when requesting a larger batch.
- **Last Video Freezes the Entire App** – Reaching the last available video may cause TikPort to freeze or become completely unresponsive.

These issues are being investigated.
